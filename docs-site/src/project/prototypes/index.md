# Prototypes

Eight small features built on 26 September 2026 to show what could help archivists get started with Clio-X.
On 28 September 2026 all eight were merged into the trial portal, https://cliox.ldas.jp ([PR #1](https://github.com/nakamura196/ClioX-mvg-portal/pull/1)). They are still prototypes: here to be tried and chosen from, not finished work.
