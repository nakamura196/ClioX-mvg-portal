# Start here

This part of the site is for archivists and records professionals who are new to Clio-X.
You do not need a technical background.

## What Clio-X is, in one paragraph

Clio-X is a place where archives can describe their records and share them under their own conditions.
Each record gets a public entry, like a catalogue card that anyone can read.
The entry says who holds the records and on what terms they may be used.
Some records can be _analysed without leaving the institution_: a researcher sends an analysis, it runs where the records are, and only the result goes back.
Every registration, change and granted request is written into a shared ledger that nobody can quietly edit.

## You cannot break anything by looking

Reading the catalogue, searching and opening a record's page change nothing.
You do not need an account or a wallet for any of that.
Only a few actions write to the ledger (publishing, editing, requesting access), and each one asks for your confirmation first.
