## Where things stand (26 September 2026)

- A trial copy of Clio-X runs at **[cliox.ldas.jp](https://cliox.ldas.jp)**. It uses Sepolia, a test network where nothing costs real money.
- One sample document was published and analysed there end to end: the analysis ran on the service, and only its result came back.
- On the way we found that records stay tied to the service that published them. If that service closes, the records disappear from the catalogue. [Read the note for archivists](/archivists/when-a-node-closes).
- The trial is run by one researcher as a study. It is not an official Clio-X service and may be stopped.
